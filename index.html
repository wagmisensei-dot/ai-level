Each domain is scored 0–100. Weight = its rough share of all human work. before today today's XP

THE MATH

LEVEL = ½ × Breadth + ½ × Weakest-link

Breadth = weighted average of the domain scores. Shows how much AI can do overall.

Weakest-link = weighted geometric mean. A low domain (like robotics) drags it down hard. Both hit 100 only when every domain is 100, so maxing out digital work alone can't reach Level 100.

👑 Level 100 = AI TAKES OVER. Every skill tree is maxed: no private companies, software products or human manual labor needed.

HOW A DROP EARNS XP

Each new feature is rated 1–5 on:

  • Reach: how many people can use it today
  • Leap: how big a jump over what came before
  • Replace: how much paid software or labor it makes optional

Impact = 0.4·Reach + 0.4·Leap + 0.2·Replace

XP = 10 × (Impact/5)² × (1 − domain score/100)

The squared impact makes big drops count far more than small ones. The last term means each point is harder to earn as a domain approaches mastery.

Rarity: Impact ≥ 4.2 LEGENDARY ≥ 3.4 EPIC ≥ 2.6 RARE, otherwise COMMON
